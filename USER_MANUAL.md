# Hay Hustle - User Manual

What to click for every action in the game. The same manual is inside the game: click **Manual** (bottom right) or press **H**.

## 1. Controls

**MOUSE (main way to play)**  
**Left-click anything** on the farm and your farmer walks there and does the action.  
Move the mouse over something first - a tooltip by the cursor says what the click will do ("Click: Refill water bucket").  
**Left-click the ground** - walk there.  
**Right mouse button + drag** - rotate the camera.     **Mouse wheel** - zoom.  
  
**BUTTONS (bottom right)**  
**Shop** - animals, helpers, workers, fields, fences, traps, sell animals.  
**Orders** - villager orders.   **Rewards** - daily login reward.   **Manual** - this guide.   **Menu** - pause & settings.  
  
**KEYBOARD (optional)**  
**W A S D** / arrows - walk     **Shift** - run     **E** - use the closest thing  
**F** or **Space** - swing the net     **B** Shop   **O** Orders   **R** Rewards   **H** Manual   **Esc** Menu / close  
  
**"ARE YOU SURE?" POPUPS**  
Buying, selling, hiring and other important actions ask you to **Confirm** or **Cancel** first.  
You can turn this off in Menu > Settings > Confirm buys > Don't ask.  
  
**ON SCREEN**  
Top left: farm, coins, water, Farm Rank, warehouse space, animals. Below: your goods.  
Top right: day and time, WANTED product, predators caught and animals lost. Right side: news.  
A yellow **!** above an animal means it is hungry (red = starving).

## 2. Water & Grass

**1. Get water**  
**Click the WELL** (stone well in the yard). Your farmer cranks the bucket and it fills up: "+10 water".  
  
**2. Plant grass**  
**Click an empty brown plot** inside a fenced field. Each plot uses **1 water**.  
If the bucket is empty, the tooltip says so - go back to the well.  
  
**3. Animals eat by themselves**  
Hungry animals walk to the nearest grass in their field and eat it down. Keep planting!  
  
**Hunger**  
Yellow ! above an animal = hungry: it produces at half speed and sells for less.  
Red ! = starving: after 45 seconds it dies.  
  
**Tips**  
Grass planted near chickens grows thicker. Cows make the grass under them grow back.  
Hire a **Water Carrier** (Shop > Workers) to replant plots for you.

## 3. Collecting

**Collect goods**  
Animals drop goods on the ground - eggs, wool, milk, goat milk, truffles, feathers, llama wool. Each item has a **glowing yellow ring** under it so you can spot it.  
**Click the item** - your farmer walks over and picks it up: "+1 Egg". (Walking over it also works.)  
  
**Spoiling**  
When an item starts **blinking**, it is about to spoil - grab it! Spoiled food attracts rats.  
  
**Warehouse**  
Everything goes into the warehouse. When it is full you can't pick anything up.  
**Click the WAREHOUSE** (grey-blue building, east side) to open it: upgrade it (+20 space) or Quick Sell goods.  
  
Hire a **Collector** (Shop > Workers) to pick up goods for you.

## 4. Buildings

**How to use a building**  
1. Have the needed goods in your warehouse.  
2. **Click the building** - it starts making the product ("Making Cake... 15s").  
3. When it is done, **click it again** to collect ("+1 Cake"). It starts the next one automatically if you have enough goods.  
  
**The ball on the roof**:  White = waiting   Yellow = working   Green = ready   Grey = locked  
  
**Buildings**  
Bakery (Rank 2): 2 Eggs -> Cake (15s)  
Weaving Mill (Rank 3): 1 Wool -> Yarn (20s)  
Dairy (Rank 5): 1 Milk -> Butter (25s)  
Cheese Maker (Rank 7): 2 Goat Milk -> Cheese (30s)  
Pillow Factory (Rank 11): 3 Feathers -> Pillow (30s)  
Tailor (Rank 14): 2 Llama Wool -> Luxury Coat (45s)  
  
Made products are worth much more. A **Factory Hand** (Shop > Workers) runs the buildings for you.

## 5. Selling

**1. Truck (best price)**  
**Click the blue TRUCK** (on the road north of the yard). For each product: **+1**, **+All** or **Return**.  
Click **Send truck** and confirm. It comes back after 24 seconds with your coins. 20 items per trip.  
  
**2. Quick Sell (instant, 70% price)**  
**Click the WAREHOUSE** -> **Sell 1** or **Sell all**. You get the coins right away.  
  
**3. Orders (bonus price)**  
Deliver villager orders at the Order Board for about 1.6x the normal price.  
  
**WANTED product**: every farm day one product sells for **double** (top right).  
  
**Sell animals**: Shop -> **Sell animals** -> **Sell 1**. Prime and rare animals are worth more.  
  
A **Driver** (Shop > Workers) loads and sends the truck for you, and keeps enough raw goods for your buildings.

## 6. Animals

**Buy animals**  
Shop -> **Animals**. A baby is cheap but must grow first. An adult produces right away. Each kind lives in its own field - buy the field first (see Fields).  
  
**Growth**: Baby -> Adult -> **Prime** (well fed for about 2.5 minutes, +50% production).  
  
**Breeding**  
Chickens: **click the NEST** (straw circle next to the well). Uses 1 egg. A chick hatches after 25s (small chance of a Golden Hen!).  
Sheep and cows: **click the BREEDING BARN** (red barn, Rank 4) with 2 well-fed adults of the same kind. Baby after 40s. Two Prime parents: 10% rare baby.  
  
**Animals and perks**  
Up to 5 of each kind (one field each).  
Chicken (Rank 1) - Eggs. Grass near chickens grows thicker.  
Sheep (Rank 3) - Wool. Hears predators early (+1.5s warning).  
Cow (Rank 5) - Milk. Makes grass regrow.  
Goat (Rank 7) - Goat milk. Headbutts snakes and rats.  
Pig (Rank 9) - Truffles. Cleans spoiled food so fewer rats come.  
Turkey (Rank 11) - Feathers. Hawks won't dive near turkeys.  
Llama (Rank 14) - Llama wool. Kicks foxes, wolves and bears.  
  
**Rare breeds**: Golden Hen, Cloud Sheep, Brave Cow, Mountain Goat, Truffle King, Royal Turkey, Snow Llama - 2x faster, sell for 5x.

## 7. Predators

**When a predator comes**  
A warning sound plays and a message says the direction. A **red marker** at the screen edge points to it. On screen, a **red bar above it shows how many hits are left**.  
  
**How to catch it**  
**Click the predator.** Your farmer runs to it and swings the net. **Every click = 1 hit** - "HIT! 2 left" ... "CAUGHT!".  
If it grabs an animal, it runs to the forest - chase it! Hits can make it drop the animal.  
  
**Predators**  
Fox (Rank 1) - 3 hits. Steals chickens and turkeys.  
Hawk (Rank 2) - 2 hits. Hit it only while it dives low.  
Wolf (Rank 3, night) - 6 hits. Takes sheep, cows and goats.  
Snake (Rank 6) - 2 hits. Hides in tall grass, eats eggs.  
Rats (Rank 10) - 1 hit each, in groups. Steal from the warehouse - catch them to get the goods back.  
Bear (Rank 15, night) - 12 hits. Takes big animals.  
  
**Difficulty**: until you hire a **Guard** (Shop -> Workers, Rank 3), **only foxes** come, one at a time. With a Guard on duty other predators can come too, but they are weak: about half the hits to catch, and slower.  
  
No predators come during the tutorial. Night brings more of them - your farmer carries a lantern.

## 8. Defense

**Helpers** (Shop -> Helpers, up to 2 each)  
**Cat** (Rank 6, 300): guards the warehouse, catches rats and finds hidden snakes.  
**Guard Dog** (Rank 8, 900): patrols the fields, bites foxes, wolves and bears.  
  
**Fences** (Shop -> Farm)  
Wooden Fence -> **Sturdy Fence** (1200, predators 30% slower inside your fields) -> **Iron Fence** (3000, 50% slower).  
  
**Traps** (Shop -> Farm, 150 each, up to 4)  
Set automatically at the edge of your fields or by the warehouse. A fox, wolf, snake or rat that steps on one is caught at once. A bear takes 4 hits. Each trap works once.  
  
**Animals that defend**  
Goats headbutt snakes and rats. Llamas and the rare Brave Cow kick foxes, wolves and bears. Turkeys keep hawks away. Adult sheep warn you earlier.  
  
**Guards** (Shop -> Workers): hired guards chase and catch predators for you.  
  
**Lamp posts** (Shop -> **Lights**, 250 each, up to 10)  
Built around your fields and the yard. They light up by themselves at night. Predators move 40% slower near a lit lamp and leave animals in the light alone when they can.

## 9. Workers

**Hire workers**: Shop -> **Workers** -> **Hire** (confirm).  
You pay a hiring fee now and a **wage every morning**. If you can't pay the wage, the worker quits.  
Workers walk around the farm; their name and current job float above their head.  
  
**Collector** (Rank 2, hire 400, 40/day, up to 4)  
Picks up goods before they spoil.  
  
**Water Carrier** (Rank 3, hire 500, 50/day, up to 4)  
Fetches water from the well and replants empty plots in all your fields.  
  
**Factory Hand** (Rank 5, hire 800, 80/day)  
Starts products in your buildings and collects them when ready.  
  
**Driver** (Rank 6, hire 600, 60/day)  
When there are enough goods, loads the truck (WANTED first, then the most valuable) and sends it. Keeps 6 of every raw good your buildings and nest need.  
  
**Guard** (Rank 3, hire 1200, 100/day, up to 4)  
Patrols your fields with a pitchfork. Runs after any predator and fights it until it is caught (a hawk only when it dives low).  
  
**Which field do they work in?**  
Collectors, Water Carriers and Guards work in **All fields (rotation)** by default - they visit your fields one after another. In Shop -> Workers, click a worker's **Works in** button to give them one field instead (click again to go to the next field). Hire up to 4 of each, e.g. one Collector for the Chicken Run and one for the Sheep Meadow.  
  
**Dismiss one** sends a worker away (no refund).

## 10. Fields

**Every kind of animal has its own field**  
Every field is the same size: **4 x 4 grass plots** and room for **5 animals**. They sit in a neat grid behind the yard, so they are all close by.  
Your chickens live in the **Chicken Run** (yours from the start). To keep any other animal, first buy its field:  
click its **FOR SALE sign**, or Shop -> **Farm** -> **Buy**, or the **Buy field** button next to the animal in Shop -> Animals.  
  
Chicken Run - chickens, free (front left)  
Sheep Meadow - Rank 3, 1000 coins (front right)  
Cow Pasture - Rank 5, 2500 coins (middle left)  
Goat Hill - Rank 7, 3500 coins (middle right)  
Pig Pen - Rank 9, 5000 coins (back left)  
Turkey Yard - Rank 11, 6500 coins (back right)  
Llama Ridge - Rank 14, 9000 coins (at the back)  
  
A sign over each gate shows how many animals are inside, e.g. "Chicken Run 3/5" (red = FULL).  
Keep the plots planted so your 5 animals never go hungry - or hire a Water Carrier.

## 11. Orders

**Order Board**  
**Click the ORDER BOARD** (notice board in the yard, next to the truck) or the **Orders** button.  
Up to 3 villagers ask for goods, e.g. "5 Eggs + 2 Cakes". Each order shows what you have (green = enough, red = missing) and the time left.  
  
Click **Deliver** (confirm) to hand over the goods for the reward - about **1.6x** the normal price, plus XP.  
Missing goods are red, and below each one the order tells you **how to get it** (e.g. "Click the Bakery to make it from 2 Eggs").  
Click **Show** and a green **GO HERE** arrow guides you to the right building or field. Click **Stop** to hide it.  
Orders only ask for goods you are able to make.  
Click **Discard** to remove an order you can't do. New orders arrive every ~25 seconds.  
If the time runs out, the order disappears.

## 12. Rank & Rewards

**Farm Rank**  
Almost everything gives XP: planting, collecting, making products, selling, orders, breeding, catching predators.  
Higher rank unlocks animals, buildings, helpers, workers and fields - and new predators. Max rank 20.  
  
**Daily rewards**  
Click **Rewards** -> **Claim** once per day. The button shows **Reward!** when one is waiting.  
Day 1: 100 coins   Day 2: water + 50   Day 3: free chick   Day 4: 250 coins  
Day 5: 6 eggs   Day 6: free adult sheep   Day 7: Mystery chest (maybe a Golden Hen!)  
Missing a day resets the streak to Day 1.

## 13. Menu & Settings

**Menu** (button or Esc) pauses the game.  
  
**Resume** - back to the farm.  
**Volume** - drag the slider.  
**Graphics** - **Low** turns off shadows and runs faster on laptops. **High** looks best.  
**Confirm buys** - **Ask first** shows "Are you sure?" popups; **Don't ask** skips them for buying and selling.  
**Replay tutorial** - shows the step-by-step guide again.  
**Save & quit to title** - saves and goes back to the title screen.  
**Quit game** - saves and closes the game.  
  
**Account**: the first screen is **Sign in / Sign up** (email + password, no code or email link needed). Tick **Stay signed in** so you don't have to type it every time. **Play offline** skips signing in. Sign out from the title screen (bottom right).  
**Saving**: the game also saves automatically every 30 seconds.  
**Backups**: a copy of your farm is kept every time you click Continue and before a New Farm (the last 3). On the title screen, click **Restore backup** to go back to one of them. Your current save is kept aside first.  
On the title screen, **Continue** loads your farm. **New Farm** starts over (it asks to confirm).
