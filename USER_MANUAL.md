# Hay Hustle - User Manual

What to click for every action in the game. The same manual is inside the game: click **Manual** (bottom right) or press **H**.

## 1. Controls

**MOUSE (main way to play)**  
**Left-click anything** on the farm and your farmer walks there and does the action.  
Move the mouse over something first - a tooltip by the cursor says what the click will do ("Click: Refill water bucket").  
**Left-click the ground** - walk there.  
**Right mouse button + drag** - rotate the camera.  
**Mouse wheel** - zoom in / out.  
  
**BUTTONS (bottom right of the screen)**  
**Shop** - buy and sell animals, buy helpers.   **Rewards** - daily login reward.   **Manual** - this guide.  
  
**KEYBOARD (optional)**  
**W A S D** or **arrow keys** - walk     **Shift** (hold) - run  
**E** - use the closest thing (shown in the bar at the bottom of the screen)  
**F** or **Space** - swing the net  
**B** - Shop     **R** - Daily rewards     **H** - Manual     **Esc** - close a window  
  
**WHAT YOU SEE ON SCREEN**  
Top left: farm name, coins, water in your bucket, Farm Rank and XP, warehouse space, number of animals.  
Below it: the goods in your warehouse.  
Top right: day and time, day/night, today's WANTED product, predators caught and animals lost.  
Right side: news messages. Floating text above things shows what just happened.

## 2. Water & Grass

**1. Get water**  
**Click the WELL** (stone well with a little roof, in the yard).  
Your farmer cranks the bucket and it fills to full: "+10 water".  
  
**2. Plant grass**  
**Click an empty brown plot** inside the fenced pasture.  
Your farmer crouches and plants. Each plot uses **1 water**.  
If the bucket is empty, the tooltip says so - go back to the well.  
  
**3. Animals eat by themselves**  
Hungry animals walk to the nearest grass and eat it down. Short grass turns yellow-green, then disappears.  
Keep planting so there is always grass.  
  
**Hunger**  
Well fed: produces at full speed.  
Hungry: produces at half speed and sells for less.  
Starving for 45 seconds: the animal dies.  
  
**Tips**  
Grass planted near chickens grows thicker (Chicken perk).  
Cows make the grass under them grow back (Cow perk).

## 3. Collecting

**Collect goods**  
Animals drop goods on the ground: eggs, wool, milk, goat milk, truffles, feathers, llama wool.  
**Click the item** - your farmer walks over, bends down and picks it up: "+1 Egg".  
(Walking over an item also collects it.)  
  
**Spoiling**  
Items left on the ground spoil. When an item starts **blinking**, it is about to spoil - grab it!  
Spoiled food attracts rats.  
  
**Warehouse**  
Everything you collect goes into the warehouse (list at the top left).  
If it is full, you cannot pick anything up.  
**Click the WAREHOUSE building** (grey-blue, east side) to upgrade it: +20 space for coins.

## 4. Buildings

**How to use a building**  
1. Have the needed goods in your warehouse.  
2. **Click the building** - it starts making the product ("Making Cake... 15s").  
3. When it is done, **click the building again** to collect ("+1 Cake"). It starts the next one automatically if you have enough goods.  
  
**The ball on the roof shows the status**  
White = waiting     Yellow = working     Green = ready, click to collect     Grey = locked  
  
**Buildings**  
Bakery (Rank 2): 2 Eggs -> Cake (15s)  
Weaving Mill (Rank 3): 1 Wool -> Yarn (20s)  
Dairy (Rank 5): 1 Milk -> Butter (25s)  
Cheese Maker (Rank 7): 2 Goat Milk -> Cheese (30s)  
Pillow Factory (Rank 11): 3 Feathers -> Pillow (30s)  
Tailor (Rank 14): 2 Llama Wool -> Luxury Coat (45s)  
  
Made products are worth much more than raw goods.

## 5. Selling

**Sell goods with the truck**  
1. **Click the TRUCK** (blue truck on the road, north of the yard). The truck window opens.  
2. For each product: **+1** loads one, **+All** loads all of it, **Return** puts it back in the warehouse.  
3. Click **Send truck**. It drives to town and comes back after 24 seconds with your coins.  
The truck holds 20 items per trip.  
  
**WANTED product**  
Every farm day one product sells for **double price**. It is shown at the top right and marked WANTED x2 in the truck.  
  
**Sell animals**  
Click **Shop** (or B) -> **Sell animals** tab -> **Sell 1**.  
Prime and rare animals sell for more. Babies and hungry animals sell for less.  
  
**Other ways to earn coins**  
Catching predators (they are sold to the zoo) and daily rewards.

## 6. Animals

**Buy animals**  
Click **Shop** -> **Buy animals**. A baby is cheap but must grow up first. An adult produces right away.  
  
**Growth**  
Baby -> Adult (after its grow time) -> **Prime** (stay well fed for about 2.5 minutes). Prime animals produce 50% faster.  
  
**Breeding**  
Chickens: **click the NEST** (straw circle next to the well). Uses 1 egg from the warehouse. A chick hatches after 25s. Small chance of a Golden Hen!  
Sheep and cows: **click the BREEDING BARN** (big red barn, Rank 4). Needs 2 well-fed adults of the same kind. A baby is born after 40s. Two Prime parents have a 10% chance of a RARE baby.  
  
**Animals and perks**  
Chicken (Rank 1) - Eggs. Grass near chickens grows thicker.  
Sheep (Rank 3) - Wool. Hears predators early (+1.5s warning).  
Cow (Rank 5) - Milk. Makes grass regrow.  
Goat (Rank 7) - Goat milk. Headbutts snakes and rats.  
Pig (Rank 9) - Truffles. Cleans spoiled food so fewer rats come.  
Turkey (Rank 11) - Feathers. Hawks won't dive near turkeys.  
Llama (Rank 14) - Llama wool. Kicks foxes, wolves and bears.  
  
**Rare breeds**: Golden Hen, Cloud Sheep, Brave Cow (kicks predators), Mountain Goat, Truffle King, Royal Turkey, Snow Llama. They produce 2x faster and sell for 5x.

## 7. Predators

**When a predator comes**  
You hear a warning sound and a message says where it comes from (north / east / south / west).  
A **red marker** at the edge of the screen points to it until it is on screen.  
On screen, a **red bar above it shows how many hits are left**.  
  
**How to catch it**  
**Click the predator.** Your farmer runs to it and swings the net.  
**Every click = 1 hit.** "HIT! 2 left" - keep clicking until "CAUGHT!".  
(Or stand next to it and press F / Space.)  
If it grabs an animal it runs back to the forest - chase it! Hits can make it drop the animal.  
  
**Predators**  
Fox (Rank 1) - 3 hits. Steals chickens and turkeys.  
Hawk (Rank 2) - 2 hits. Flies high, dives for chickens. You can only hit it while it dives low.  
Wolf (Rank 3, night only) - 6 hits. Takes sheep, cows and goats.  
Snake (Rank 6) - 2 hits. Hides in tall grass (invisible until you are close) and eats eggs.  
Rats (Rank 10) - 1 hit each, come in groups. Steal goods from the warehouse. Catch them to get the goods back.  
Bear (Rank 15, night only) - 12 hits. Takes big animals.  
  
**Night is more dangerous**: more predators and wolves/bears appear. Your farmer carries a lantern.

## 8. Helpers

**Helpers fight predators for you**  
Click **Shop** -> **Helpers** -> **Buy**. You can have up to 2 of each.  
  
**Cat** (Rank 6, 300 coins)  
Waits by the warehouse. Catches rats and finds snakes hiding in the grass.  
  
**Guard Dog** (Rank 8, 900 coins)  
Patrols the pasture. Chases and bites foxes, wolves and bears.  
  
**Animals that defend themselves**  
Goats headbutt snakes and rats. Llamas and the rare Brave Cow kick foxes, wolves and bears.  
Turkeys keep hawks away. Adult sheep warn you earlier.

## 9. Rank & Rewards

**Farm Rank**  
Almost everything gives XP: planting, collecting, making products, selling, breeding, catching predators.  
Higher rank unlocks new animals, buildings and helpers - and new predators. Max rank is 20.  
  
**Daily rewards**  
Click **Rewards** (or R) -> **Claim** once per day.  
Day 1: 100 coins   Day 2: water + 50 coins   Day 3: free chick   Day 4: 250 coins  
Day 5: 6 eggs   Day 6: free adult sheep   Day 7: Mystery chest (maybe a Golden Hen!)  
Missing a day resets the streak to Day 1.  
  
**Saving**  
The game saves automatically every 30 seconds and when you quit.  
On the title screen, **Continue** loads your farm. **New Farm** starts over (click twice to confirm).
